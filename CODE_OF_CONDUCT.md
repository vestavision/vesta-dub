# Code of conduct

Be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion on the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and sexualized language are not welcome.

Do not post other people's private information. Do not upload browser profiles, cookies, or caption dumps from videos you do not have rights to share.

The maintainer may edit, lock, or reject contributions that break this standard. Report conduct concerns to security@vestavision.io. Report security vulnerabilities to the same address, using the process in `SECURITY.md`.

This is a project policy, not legal advice.
